## 3. What the portfolio still lacks for these

- **Python as the first language.** Every top-15 posting is Python-first (Inspect, verifiers, harnesses). The blind-spot Inspect port is the right start; the rest of the instruments are Node/BigInt. A pip-installable package (pyproject, tests, CI badge, versioned release on PyPI) of at least one verifier would answer Braintrust, Epoch, AISI Core Technology and Nous in one artifact.
- **Lean 4 in the hands, not "Lean-adjacent".** Harmonic (both roles), Axiom, Math Inc, Alignerr and, implicitly, Jane Street Formal Methods want proof-assistant fluency (Lean/Coq/Isabelle). One formalized certificate (e.g., a Krawczyk-box or an SOS certificate checked in Lean/Mathlib) would move three fit-4 roles to fit-5.
- **A peer-reviewed or at least arXiv-indexed publication.** DeepMind, Scale RS, Cohere RS, Redwood and IMPA weight publications; Zenodo DOIs and site reports are not read as such. The Erdős #1 note and the time-horizon curve are the two papers closest to submittable.
- **Training-side ML experience.** Research-Engineer roles at OpenAI/Anthropic/DeepMind assume PyTorch/JAX, RL post-training and multi-GPU work. The portfolio is entirely on the verification side; say so, and target evals/verifier roles rather than "Research Engineer, RL".
- **LLM-as-judge calibration, stated on his terms.** Nous, Transluce and Scale ask for judge calibration. The battery that proves two scorers agree on every mutant is a calibration protocol with a REFUSED third value; write it up as one so the keyword matches without conceding the doctrine.
- **Human baselines and a time-horizon number** (METR plan deliverables 1–2). Without them the "first time-horizon on judge-free tasks" sentence is a plan, not a result; Epoch, Mercor APEX and Anthropic Model Evaluations all read for exactly this.
- **Work authorization.** Surge, Elicit, Netflix and CAISI are US-only; Anthropic Fellows is UK/US/Canada; AISI can sponsor "any nationality" but is London hybrid. For the on-site roles a one-line relocation stance in the cover note saves a screening round.
- **Security and hardware-DV depth** for the red-team and Jump roles (SVA/UVM, 3+ years) — not worth building; skip those.
- **A one-page resume and a GitHub profile** that show tests, CI and the ~90 gated batteries as engineering practice, not only as site copy (the METR plan's deliverable 5).

OpenAI and Anthropic each published a Lean proof that the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false. I replayed both with two independent kernels: both proofs check against their own statements. A kernel can't say what a statement means, so the statements were read too.
---
Anthropic's statement is ETH's negation itself: s₃ = 0 for Mathlib's Turing machines. OpenAI's Lean defines no ETH; it proves a running-time bound. So I wrote the textbook ETH once in Lean and proved each statement refutes it, in that lab's own machine model.

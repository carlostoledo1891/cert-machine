# The Chief Scientist — WARRANT judged as mathematics (2026-10-29)

## 1. Where I stand after reading it

**The two axes are a real theorem, not a slogan.** `playground/warrant.js` separates standing (how a number was made) from verdict (what a question got), and `instruments/verdict.js` keeps the verdict algebra where it belongs: `compose` is Kleene's strong conjunction, and the brainstorm (§2, C9) puts Belnap's fourth corner in the knowledge order, emitted only by concordance. Standing is a provenance annotation, not a truth value; read the four standings as Kleene-plus-one and a dashed line becomes "probably true", which rule 1 of `design/WARRANT.md` forbids. The house refused that reading in code.

**The meet is sound, and that warrant.js implements only the meet is a virtue, not a gap.** REFUSED < CHOSEN < COMPUTED < DECIDED is a finite chain, hence a complete distributive lattice with min as meet; nothing to argue. But the chain is a *quotient* of what the glosses describe: two independent bits, E = "exact arithmetic on the path" and U = "the data determine it". DECIDED = (E,U), CHOSEN = (E,¬U), COMPUTED = (¬E,U), and the float pick (¬E,¬U) is folded into CHOSEN. Checked by a 25-case script just now (the product with a bottom adjoined): the folding map preserves every meet and fails exactly two joins, CHOSEN ⊔ COMPUTED in either order, which the chain calls COMPUTED and the product calls (E,U) — and neither derivation establishes the other's bit, so the product join is false too. "Weakest on the path" is exactly right; the brainstorm's §3.1 (max, min) provenance-semiring join across independent derivations is where the chain would start lying. Two independent derivations give the mark the standing of its best *single* derivation, never a join.

**The witness is correctly typed as an existential.** Rule 5 and C3 of the brainstorm get what most uncertainty-visualization literature gets wrong: a red × is ∃y₀ ∈ B: ¬P(y₀), the falsifier of a universal claim, and a scalar comparison refuted by an enclosure clearing a bar has no witness because there is no object. The meet specimen on `site/grammar/index.html` (2/3 at x = 1/2, (2/3)² = 4/9 ≠ 1/2) is a correct existential, exhibited and decided.

## 2. Three things I would change or add, ranked

**R-A. A standing is read from a record, never typed at a call site.** `W.attrs(W.COMPUTED, …)` lets the author choose the ink; "ink is read, never chosen" (`design/grammar.js`) is enforced one level too low. A grep today finds 91 of 149 `W.attrs`/`W.value` uses in `tools/` and `playground/` passing a standing literal (`tools/build-report-kissing.js` lines 35, 137, 313, 500 among them). The renderer should take a typed value, cert-unit's `Val = {datum, kind, hyp}`, and derive the stroke from `kind`; literals stay only for specimens (`tools/build-grammar.js` computes its own numbers). Check: a census of standing literals, ratcheted down like the dash census; it is what makes **page soundness** (drawn standing ≤ recorded, per mark) decidable from HTML plus ledger, which no gate does today (brainstorm §10). Seen on: the kissing ladder figure, where "dashed while the record rests on the queued row" is a `dashed ? COMPUTED : DECIDED` ternary written by a person.

**R-B. A pixel is rounded outward like a digit: a lower end floors, an upper end ceils, at the figure's declared resolution.** `tools/build-grammar.js` draws the decided enclosure stair with `f.px(x).toFixed(1)` on both ends; `toFixed` rounds to nearest, so an upper end moves inward half the time. The amount is invisible; the principle is the truncated-upper-bound lesson reappearing in pixels. Rule 8 already says "round a printed bound outward, never truncate"; extend it to extents, and have every figure print its resolution (one pixel in data units, the brainstorm's G3, target `vis-figure-resolution-claim` OPEN since 09-04). Check: a gate reads each extent's endpoints and the record's enclosure and decides containment exactly. Seen on: the DECIDED specimen on `/grammar/`.

**R-C. A premise grade is a pair, not a rung: a verification bit and a validation rung.** The ladder assumed < verified < compared < validated is not a chain in the literature it cites. Roache and ASME V&V 20 make verification (the code solves its equations) and validation (the equations describe the thing) orthogonal: a validated-but-unverified model agrees with the world by compensating errors, and a verified-but-unvalidated one solves the wrong equations exactly. Swell's chain on `/grammar/` puts the open-sea band on the top rung, "validated", while the same page says "verified: none yet"; `W.weakest` as a chain min cannot express that. Keep the words, keep the glyph; make `grade` = (verified?, {assumed, compared, validated}) with weakest = componentwise min, and print "unverified" where the bit is off. Check: `W.weakest` over the product, and a build that refuses a chain whose top rung lacks the bit without the word. Seen on: `site/grammar/index.html`, the premise chain, and `site/swell/index.html`.

## 3. The five questions

**Rebuild a report page from paper, folder or link.** The grammar is a map ρ from records to marks; a rebuild is ρ⁻¹, well-posed only when the page is sound and complete and each mark points at its record. Mechanism: a `data-rec` (record path, sha, field) on every `w-*` mark, so a built page is a serialisation of its ledger; a PDF whose figures are SVG keeps it, a raster does not. From a stranger's paper it is not the grammar's job: a solid line in a journal is a convention, not a decision, and reading it into standings is claimbox's work (a READING hole, §3.6).

**LabECO–Petrobras.** The ladder is the V&V vocabulary their reviewers already demand, so the print kit (`design/print/PROPOSAL.md`) forces the premise chain to be written, which is the academic deliverable. For a design wave the mathematics is honest: pinned data (DECIDED) through a chosen family (CHOSEN) through a fit (COMPUTED) meets to CHOSEN, with the certified return-level enclosure drawn as its own extent, as `tools/video-tail.js` gates. Failure mode: an engineer reading dotted as "less precise"; the legend's gloss is the only defence, and R-C makes the chain say what their standard says.

**A frontier research topic.** Yes, in the narrow decidable form: sound figures, drawn ≤ recorded over a whole site, with the census as the finding. Prior art: Tupper 2001 (three-valued pixels by intervals), Melquiond's verified plotting, Kindlmann and Scheidegger 2014 (commutative diagrams for visualization). New is an *order*, not a symmetry, checked against a record rather than an algorithm. A perception claim needs a study; the mathematics does not.

**Crossing it with core concepts.** Render is an abstraction α: records → marks, reading a concretisation γ: marks → the records that could have produced them; soundness is r ∈ γ(α(r)), a Galois connection, Cousot's abstract interpretation applied to evidence (brainstorm §13 names it; it should be the spine). Outward rounding is the same condition on extents. The hypothesis travelling with the number is a judgment Γ ⊢ v : s with Γ the union along the path; rule 6's italic *given* is Γ printed, and a build can check that "what it rests on" equals that union. Lakatos's forward support and backward refutation (C7) is the composition law the register still lacks edges for.

**"A system that by its own construction is the math itself."** Precisely: the page is a certificate of a structure-preserving map, not the theorem. Soundness: no mark stronger than its record (standing, extent, verdict word). Completeness: every ledger row of the page is a mark or ∅ with a word. Both decidable at build. Today `/grammar/`'s specimens are sound by construction and the reports by discipline; the sentence is earned when a gate decides it.

## 4. An objection, to Tufte

He will call the dashed underline on every computed number non-data ink and the hatched ∅ a chartjunk field. My answer: the underline carries one bit the digits cannot, and on this site that bit *is* the finding (a 592 printed, never decided, beside four 604s decided). Provenance ink is data ink. He will reply that the ratio is about the quantity shown and that provenance belongs in a footnote; I expect the house to hold, because a footnote is not local (law G4) and the 605 lesson is what a reading in prose costs.

## 5. Adopt by name, refuse by name

Adopt **the Galois connection**: say "render is α, read is γ, soundness is r ∈ γ(α(r))" in WARRANT.md; it names the gate the grammar still owes and gives every lossy view (digits, pixels, a page's standing, an older legend) one law. Refuse **a join of standings**: never compute max over the chain; two independent derivations give the mark the standing of the best one, said in words, because the chain is a meet-homomorphic image of the real structure and not a join-homomorphic one.

- **T7 (witness typing).** Every red × is attached to a universal claim and an object of the same record.

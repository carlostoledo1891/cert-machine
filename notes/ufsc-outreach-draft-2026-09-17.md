**Subject:** your 16,369 breaking waves, read number by number — and three more pages on your line

Pedro —

Your GRL paper came out yesterday (16 Sept). I had been reading the Zenodo table
since January without a paper beside it, so I read the paper against the table
the day it appeared, in exact arithmetic:

- https://carlostoledo.co/reports/breaking-geometry.html — every printed number
  decided against the per-event table with your scripts' own definitions. The
  three quadratic laws (0.83, 0.66, 0.20 cb²/g) and the 4 ± 2 % self-similar
  tail reproduce to the digit. Three things you may want to know before anyone
  else asks: the θ range "3° to 71°" is not the θ column of the table (1.38° to
  42.97°) but the integer parts of atan(Δz/eB); Table 1's Hs is the pickle's
  column `sv_fp2`, and the column the pickle names `Hs` is something else
  (0.20–0.75, multiples of 1/256); and "Ab/L²_D81 = 0.22" is the slope for the
  area at the frame of maximum length, not the maximum area the histogram uses.
  The stratified-by-sea-state analysis your §5 calls beyond scope is on the
  page, per record: g·Lb/cb² runs 0.66–1.11 across the twenty records and
  ranks with Hs at ρ = −0.49, with wave age at −0.03.
- https://carlostoledo.co/reports/return-levels.html — the method of your
  Ocean Engineering paper (block maxima, five families, Anderson–Darling,
  100-year levels) on three NDBC buoys with every fit certified as the unique
  MLE in a box. EW wins the hourly series on two buoys of three; the Weibull
  never wins a block size; the lognormal wins every annual series. If you can
  share one grid point's series I will run the same certificate on it.
- https://carlostoledo.co/instruments/stereo-reach/ — the error budget of a
  stereo rig as enclosures, with your Leme 2020 and Caparica 2021 rigs as
  presets. At Caparica the 27–55 % area-minus-point Hs excess in Table 2 is
  inside what the rig's own quantisation cell produces.
- https://carlostoledo.co/reports/ec-benchmark.html — the environmental-contour
  benchmark re-decided to the last point (173 of 176 printed counts exact; 30
  contours that cross themselves).

Everything is exact or an enclosure; anything that is a choice is marked as
one; nothing is peer-reviewed. Corrections welcome — the pages are built from
records and a wrong record is a one-line fix.

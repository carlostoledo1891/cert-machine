# Locking rules — reviewed and decided, 2026-10-05

The operator, 2026-10-05: "The locking rules must be reviewed and maybe dropped. Make a chase and decide."

The test is the repository's own, from CLAUDE.md and the memory note on the unlocked fork. A rule that
**measures a fact or protects the record** stays. A rule that **refuses a direction** goes. A scope judgement
that was measured stays as information, never as a ban, and is reopened when the facts change.

Sources read: CLAUDE.md, HANDOFF.md (the standing-rule blocks), notes/positioning-decisions-2026-09-03.md,
the pre-registrations, the environment READMEs, and the session memory notes.

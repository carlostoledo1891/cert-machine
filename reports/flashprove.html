FlashProve, checked from the public bytes

FlashProve (Anthropic, released 30 October 2026 as a research artefact) lets a model provider prove that a reply came from the model it committed to, with a soundness theorem in Lean 4. Here a second party ran its verifier, its Lean library and its GPU route from the public repository at 9e2bdd8f, read its statements against what its theorems cover, and recomputed its numbers exactly. No tampered proof was accepted. The gaps below are already in FlashProve's own ledger; they sit between the README's sentences and that ledger.

§1 · the gaps

What the headline sentences leave out

Two readers read the README, the theorem file, the limits and gaps ledgers and the paper, each without seeing the other's table. A gap counts only if both found it. Every one is dated at the pin, 9e2bdd8f, and every one is already stated somewhere in FlashProve's own ledger; the column on the right says where.

gapwhere the claimant states it
G1Nothing is proved about the programs a user runs. Soundness is a theorem about the Lean verify under a random oracle. It is not about cp_verify, and the hash is not BLAKE2b. README.md:15-16 tells users to check with cp_verify, next to the bound.LIMITS L1, L4, L8; GAPS 45, 51; THEOREM.md:199-201
G2"The committed model" means the weights an extractor reads. No theorem ties them to the provider's model or to cp_commit. No theorem ties a commitment ID to one set of public files, so one ID could pass with two file sets and two sets of weights.LIMITS L2, L28; GAPS 64, 69, 77; THEOREM.md item 4
G3The bound "q/2^124 + 2^-101" holds under conditions the README sentence does not give. It holds only at exact match, which verify does not test, while serving uses the clipped-gap policy. It holds only for files fixed before the hash is drawn, and it is the probability that one proof is both accepted and wrong. N proofs at once cost N times; proofs checked one after another have no bound.LIMITS L4, L5; GAPS 5, 6, 44, 70; THEOREM.md:86, :300-303
G4No real deployment is shown to satisfy the theorems. Completeness and zero knowledge hold for the Lean prover, on requests in CanServe. They are known to have content at one Lean value, storedSetup, whose scale file comes from synthetic weights. No proof on real weights has gone to the Lean verifier, and there is no theorem about the GPU prover.THEOREM.md:41-44; GAPS 14, 52, 57; LIMITS L27, L69
G5Weight hiding is proved only of the Lean prover, and it counts something else. The zero-knowledge count of 688,127 is single-request replies on one copy of the table. The programs stop a copy at 688,127 batches of up to 64 requests, and no theorem ties the two counts. The proof assumes fresh randomness, while the programs derive masks from a seed. The paper calls hiding by the programs "our judgement, not a proof".LIMITS L83, L84, L93; GAPS 15, 15(3); paper abstract

One point a single reader raised was confirmed against the text: the small example that route 1 verifies is outside every theorem (THEOREM.md: "The small example's files are out of scope: verify rejects them"). A YES on it is a fact about the C++ program, not an instance of the soundness theorem. The merged reading is corpus/flashprove/readings/merged.md.

§4 · limits

What this check is, and is not

  • Not a review of the cryptography. The theorems were checked to compile and, where the build finished, to rest on the standard axioms; their mathematics is the kernels', not ours. Nothing here is a security assessment.
  • Route 4 untouched. The 400B prover on real model weights was out of scope; no claim about it is decided here.
  • Pre-registered. Every step and its expected result were written and pushed before any run (notes/flashprove-preregistration-2026-11-04.md). No step was re-run to change its outcome.
  • Spend. Route 1: GitHub-hosted runner (free). Lean pod: ≈ US$12 (cpu3m 32 vCPU, about 6.9 h). GPU pod: ≈ US$1.81 (H100, 21 min).
  • Our own error, recorded. The second check of the fresh GPU proof did not run because our script passed wrong file paths; the proof was deleted with the pod. It is listed as NOT RUN, not as anything the claim did.

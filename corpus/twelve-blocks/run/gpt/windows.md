# Eleven windows of radius 2m

**Theorem.** At every integer scale m ≥ 1, permit arbitrary flips in the eleven integer intervals (bm−2m,bm+2m], where b runs through the unit-scale interior boundaries. The minimum change in monochromatic progressions is zero. At even m only the empty mask attains it. At odd m precisely the 51 labeled masks below attain it. Thus widening the original 22 sites to 44m sites adds no ties.

Number the boundaries 1 through 11 in increasing order. One odd-scale mask is empty. Eight masks swap the two sites at a single boundary, at index 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 or 11. The remaining 42 each move a consecutive run of boundaries by one site in the same direction. For each starting index, the table gives the allowed last indices. Both directions are allowed in every row. These masks are distinct, with no quotient by reflection or color exchange.

## Pair interactions

Use baseline signs s(p) equal to +1 on the first block. Write L(p) for the change from flipping p alone. Expanding the monochromatic indicator (1+ab+ac+bc)/4 in flip bits gives the change for S as the sum of L(p) for p in S plus the sum of s(p)s(q)w(p,q) over unordered distinct pairs in S. Here w counts the same-parity test and the two legal extrapolations 2p−q and 2q−p. In particular −2s(p)L(p) is the sum over q≠p of s(q)w(p,q).

Let K(i,j) be the number of the two values 2b_i−b_j and 2b_j−b_i strictly between 0 and 548. Every one of these values is at least 6 away from 0 and 548. Moving sites in the stated windows perturbs a scaled value by strictly less than 6m, so extrapolation counts are constant, even for two sites of the same window. K(i,i)=2. The windows are disjoint.

For each window let z_i be the sum of old signs of its flipped sites and v_i the sum of the old signs multiplied by (−1)^p. The total signed even and odd charges are (Σz±Σv)/2. Adding the diagonal to the pair sum adds weight 3 at each flipped site. The exact cost becomes

    4D = 2 Σ_{p in S}(2L(p)−3) + 2 zᵀKz + (Σz)² + (Σv)².

## Compressing the choices in one window

Partition each window into four sequences, by side of its boundary and absolute site parity. Within each sequence, let k=0,1,…,m−1 index sites outwards from the boundary. Their single-site costs satisfy

    2L(p)−3 = H_i(2k+1) + β_i s(p)(−1)^p.

In index order H=(2,2,3,2,3,3,3,2,3,2,2). At odd m the vector β is (−3,−1,−4,−3,−4,0,−4,−3,−4,−1,−3), and at even m it is (−1,1,−2,1,−2,2,−2,1,−2,1,−1).

Here is how the finite core proves the formula for unbounded m. Put F(t)=Σ_{1≤q≤min(t,n)}s(q). The signed neighborhood sum with its diagonal included is

    P + F(2p−1) − F(2p−n−1) + F(⌊(n+p)/2⌋) − F(⌊p/2⌋),

where P counts signs at sites of the same parity as p. This follows by summing each of the three completion tests, including q=p each time. Multiplication by −s(p) gives 2L(p)−3. Each block (am,cm] contributes its sign times (t−am)₊−(t−cm)₊ to F(t). On fixed scale parity, side and sequence, each floor argument is affine in (m,k), as is P by parity counting. Let m₀=1 or 2 be the first scale of the chosen parity. The region m≥m₀, 0≤k≤m−1 is the sum of the segment joining (m₀,0) to (m₀,m₀−1) and the cone generated by (2,0),(2,2). To prove a positive part takes one affine branch throughout, check its two endpoint values and its two increments along these rays have a common weak sign. The core does this for every prefix argument and boundary, then checks the discrepancy in the claimed formula vanishes at the endpoints and along both rays.

For fixed signed charge u in one parity at one boundary, let l and r be the left and right flip counts. Then |u|=|l−r|. The first l or r terms of the corresponding sequence minimize their contribution strictly, since H_i>0. The odd numbers sum to a square, so the part without β is at least H_i(l²+r²)≥H_i u². Equality occurs exactly when one side is empty and the other is an initial segment. The β term depends only on the signed charges. Thus a unique compact mask minimizes at any attainable (z,v). Each integer charge of absolute value at most m is attainable. For a lower bound we may drop these capacity constraints, retaining z_i≡v_i modulo 2.

## An exact eleven-dimensional certificate

Set A=2K+11ᵀ+diag(H), where 11ᵀ denotes the all-ones matrix. After compression, use u_even=(z_i+v_i)/2 and u_odd=(z_i−v_i)/2. Four times the compact cost equals

    zᵀAz + (Σv−h)² − h² + Σ [H_i v_i² + 2γ_i v_i],

with h=2 at odd m, h=0 at even m, γ_i=β_i+h. In both cases |γ_i|=H_i−1. For an integer v_i of the parity prescribed by z_i, the bracket is at least −(H_i−2)(z_i mod 2). This follows by checking 0, ±1 and observing that every |v_i|≥2 has positive bracket. Consequently D≤0 requires zᵀAz≤h²+5. Exact rational LDLᵀ proves A positive definite. Enumerating its successive squares from the last coordinate gives 213 vectors in zᵀAz≤9 and 21 in zᵀAz≤5, with coordinates and signs fully labeled.

For each z the core tries all v_i of matching parity with remaining budget. After adding (H_i−2)(z_i mod 2) to each bracket, all costs are nonnegative and the budget is at most 9. Every |v_i|≥5 exceeds it. The core checks the remaining square (Σv−h)², reconstructs the compact masks, and asserts exact equality to the table and empty even-scale mask. All reconstructed charges fit already at m=1 on odd scales and m=2 on even scales. Reconstruction depends solely on scale parity and the index of a site in its sequence. A noncompact mask has strictly higher cost at the same charges.

Replay from this directory with `python3 -B window_core.py`. Only the Python standard library is needed. A fixed symmetric radius r is covered as soon as m≥⌈r/2⌉. The entire flip-cost function for that fixed window then repeats with scale parity, by the pair and local formulas above.

The finite comparison here imposes a support restriction. A coloring with flips outside these windows requires a further argument.

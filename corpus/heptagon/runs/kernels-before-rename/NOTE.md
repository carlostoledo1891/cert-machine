# The kernels before the rename

The verdict logs of the four second kernels on the same proofs, run before the library's namespace was renamed for
publication. Files without a prefix are the heptagon closure, `g-` the Theorem A.3 closure, `s-` the corollary's
closure; `-env` the heptagon's whole environment, `-union` the whole environment of both roots (nanoda's
`nanoda-union-r2` with the environment's 15 core axioms permitted). Only verdict logs are kept here: the other logs of
those runs carry the old name.

One line omitted: `s-con-leche.log` lacks its third line, con-leche's timing statistics ("slowest checks: …"), which
names a declaration under the old name. The verdict line is unchanged. The full log's sha256 is
`27babaff7a9c4af581b485314a7c80d8a85cf89955a76072ad0036af03ac3d2b`.

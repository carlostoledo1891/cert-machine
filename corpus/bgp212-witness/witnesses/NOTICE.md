# The witness files

`axiom212_k45_d21.json`, `axiom212_k45_d20.json` and `axiom212_k45_d21_exact_ratio.txt` are copied unchanged from
Fredrik Prüzelius, [pruzze/bounded-gaps-witnesses](https://github.com/pruzze/bounded-gaps-witnesses), commit
`92000064b5e983761dd751a3e0a62ce83f6d2c7b` (2026-11-16, "Independent witness for the numerical certificate of
H_1 <= 212"), announced on [AxiomMath/BGP212 issue #2](https://github.com/AxiomMath/BGP212/issues/2). His README
licenses the witness files under **CC BY 4.0**; they are redistributed here under that licence, with this attribution.
Nothing of his code was copied, read or run: see `instruments/bgp212-witness/README.md`. Each file's sha256 is pinned
in `corpus/bgp212-witness/PROVENANCE.json`.

# Smooth isometric immersions of surfaces into ℝ<sup>4</sup>

**333. Smooth isometric immersions of surfaces into ℝ<sup>4</sup>.** Every closed smooth Riemannian surface admits a smooth isometric immersion into ℝ<sup>4</sup>, resolving the closed-surface form of the four-dimensional isometric-immersion problem. This includes nonorientable surfaces and metrics of arbitrary Gaussian curvature. ([Lean](lean/docs/333.md))

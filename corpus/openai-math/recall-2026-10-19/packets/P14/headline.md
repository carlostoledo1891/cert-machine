# Borsuk's conjecture fails in dimension nine

**156. Borsuk's conjecture fails in dimension nine.** Constructs a compact subset of ℝ<sup>9</sup> that cannot be covered by ten sets of strictly smaller diameter, disproving Borsuk's covering assertion already in dimension nine. The example consists of rank-one orthogonal projectors onto lines in ℝ<sup>4</sup>, with the Frobenius metric. ([Lean](lean/docs/156.md))

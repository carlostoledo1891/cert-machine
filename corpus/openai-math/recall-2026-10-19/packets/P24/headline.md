# Uniformly bounded components of Gaussian-prime graphs

**028. Uniformly bounded components of Gaussian-prime graphs.** Proves the Gaussian moat conjecture: no infinite walk through distinct Gaussian primes can have uniformly bounded steps. More strongly, for every distance bound <i>D</i>, the graph joining Gaussian primes at distance at most <i>D</i> has uniformly bounded finite component sizes, depending only on <i>D</i>, including primes on the coordinate axes.

# Independent largest prime factors of consecutive integers

**012. Independent largest prime factors of consecutive integers.** Resolves the Erdős–Pomerance joint Dickman conjecture: the logarithmic sizes of the largest prime factors of <i>n</i> and $`n+1`$ are asymptotically independent in ordinary natural density. In particular, the integers satisfying $`P^+(n)\lt P^+(n+1)`$ have density 1/2. ([Lean](lean/docs/012.md))

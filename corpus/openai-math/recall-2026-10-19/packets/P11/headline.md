# A counterexample to periodic tiling in dimension three

**155. A counterexample to periodic tiling in dimension three.** Constructs a finite translational tile in ℤ<sup>3</sup> that tiles space but admits no fully periodic tiling, disproving the periodic tiling conjecture in the smallest possible lattice dimension. Its unit-cube thickening gives the same counterexample in ℝ<sup>3</sup>, even with arbitrary real translation vectors. ([Lean](lean/docs/155.md))

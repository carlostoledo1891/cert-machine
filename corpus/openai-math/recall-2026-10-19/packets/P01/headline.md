# Positive lower density of large prime gaps

**026. Positive lower density of large prime gaps.** For every fixed <i>C</i> &gt; 0, a positive proportion of consecutive prime gaps exceed $`C\log p_n`$, throughout every sufficiently large initial segment of the primes. The proportion may depend on <i>C</i>. Consequently, the indices where $`p_n/n`$ increases have positive lower density, answering Erdős and Prachar. ([Lean](lean/docs/026.md))

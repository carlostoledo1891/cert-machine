# The sharp terminal leave in random triangle removal

**188. The sharp terminal leave in random triangle removal.** Starting from the complete graph on <i>n</i> vertices, repeatedly delete a uniformly chosen remaining triangle. The terminal edge count is asymptotic to $`n^{3/2}/(2\sqrt2)`$, with mean-square convergence after normalization by <i>n</i><sup>3/2</sup>. This proves the triangle case of the Joos–Kühn sharp-constant conjecture. ([Lean](lean/docs/188.md))

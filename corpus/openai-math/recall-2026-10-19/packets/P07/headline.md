# Boolean functions violate the square-root degree bound by arbitrary factors

**192. Boolean functions violate the square-root degree bound by arbitrary factors.** Disproves the proposed square-root bound relating a Boolean function's linear Fourier coefficients to its polynomial degree. For every <i>C</i> &gt; 0, there is a sign-valued Boolean function <i>f</i> with $`\sum_i\widehat f(\{i\})\gt C\sqrt{\deg(f)}`$. Thus its total signed correlation with individual input bits can exceed the proposed bound by an arbitrary factor. ([Lean](lean/docs/192.md))

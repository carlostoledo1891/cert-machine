# Cycle–clique Ramsey numbers

**189. Cycle–clique Ramsey numbers.** Proves the Erdős–Faudree–Rousseau–Schelp conjecture: $`R(C_m,K_n)=(m-1)(n-1)+1`$ for every $`m\ge n\ge3`$, except $`R(C_3,K_3)=6`$. This is the exact threshold forcing a red <i>m</i>-cycle or a blue <i>n</i>-clique in every red–blue coloring of a complete graph. ([Lean](lean/docs/189.md))

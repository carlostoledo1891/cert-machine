# A counterexample to Kaplansky’s zero-divisor conjecture

**196. A counterexample to Kaplansky’s zero-divisor conjecture.** Constructs a finitely presented torsion-free group <i>G</i> whose group algebra $`\mathbb F_2[G]`$ has nonzero zero divisors, disproving Kaplansky's zero-divisor conjecture. The group has a finite two-dimensional classifying space. ([Lean](lean/docs/196.md))

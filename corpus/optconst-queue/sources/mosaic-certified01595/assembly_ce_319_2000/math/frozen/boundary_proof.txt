## 3. Exact phases and the moment reconstruction

For w off the logarithm cut, define

    alpha(w) = 1/(2w) + 1/(w-1) + Log(w/(2pi))/2,
    M_0(w) = [w(w-1)/16] pi^(-w/2) sqrt(2pi)
             * exp((w/2-1/2)Log(w/2)-w/2),
    M_t(w) = M_0(w) exp(t alpha(w)^2/4),
    D(w) = sum_(n=1)^N exp(t log(n)^2/4) n^(-w-t alpha(w)/2),
    gamma = M_t(s)/M_t(h).

Polymath's finite approximation is exactly f_t=D(h)+gamma D(s). Indeed s_*=h+t alpha(h)/2 and kappa=t[alpha(s)-alpha(conj(h))]/2, so

    conj(s_*) + kappa - y = s + t alpha(s)/2.

This identity is checked symbolically with independent alpha symbols, rather than tested numerically. The code always uses full complex alpha. On the real axis, if eta=(t/2)Im(alpha((1+iX)/2)), the two chirality phases are **plus and minus (X/2+eta)log(n)**, not plus and minus (X/2)log(n). Neither eta nor its derivative is dropped. At the working lower time the checker also gates a nonzero eta as a representation check; this sample is not a continuum proof.

Put n0=N/2=345494, l=log(n0), theta=(X+1/2)/2, L_n=log(n/n0). The banked exact moments are

    S_k = sum_(n=1)^N n^(-1/2) exp(i theta log(n)) L_n^k,
    C[e,j] = S_(e+2j)/(e! j! 4^j),  0<=e,j<=61.

Their two chunks cover every integer 1<=n<=N exactly once. The script forms interval coefficients by exact rational endpoint addition and division. For each of the 7688 real/imaginary components, the banked printed leading-digit identity is rechecked; the printed value never replaces the coefficient interval.

Define

    P_t(z) = sum_(e=0)^61 sum_(j=0)^61 C[e,j] z^e t^j,
    b = -y/2 - i/4 - t alpha(h)/2,
    a =  y/2 - i/4 - t alpha(conj(s))/2,

## 4. Structural simplification: direct whole-box gamma

The polynomial prefactors cancel identically: s(s-1)=h(h-1). Thus

    gamma = exp(-(s-h)(log(pi)+1)/2
                +(s/2-1/2)Log(s/2)-(h/2-1/2)Log(h/2)
                +t(alpha(s)^2-alpha(h)^2)/4).

This is the ratio of the two explicitly defined M_t factors, not a choice of a global logarithm of gamma. It is valid with their original principal logarithms; s/2 and h/2 stay far into opposite open half-planes, away from the cuts. ## 8. PT2021 initialization and why a zero cannot cross

The accepted published input is Platt--Trudgian, *The Riemann hypothesis is true up to 3*10^12*, Bull. London Math. Soc. (2021), arXiv:2004.09765, at the campaign's banked exact endpoint T_PT=3000175332800. Its consumed statement, already an input of V1-H-213, is:

    every nontrivial zeta zero beta+i*gamma with 0<gamma<=T_PT
    has beta=1/2.

Negative ordinates follow by conjugation; there is no nontrivial zero of ordinate zero. Only reality is needed; we assert or consume no simplicity or zero-count supplement. This package does not re-run or newly audit the PT computation. The exact endpoint above is the accepted banked interface, not a rounding of the paper title's height.

Polymath's normalization is H_0(z)=xi((1+iz)/2)/8. For a nontrivial zero rho=beta+i*gamma, the inverse coordinate is

    z=2*gamma+i*(1-2*beta).

It is real exactly when beta=1/2, and |Re(z)|<=X implies |gamma|<=X/2. The affine map has nonzero derivative, so multiplicities are preserved but need not be one. The code symbolically verifies both coordinate components and checks

    X/2 = 6000000185827/2 < T_PT,
    T_PT-X/2 = 350479773/2 > 0.

Hence all H_0 zeros with |Re(z)|<=X are real, without any RH input above PT2021. At x=0 there is also the direct positivity H_0(iy)>0 from the positive kernel.

A continuous zero trajectory can cross a stationary vertical line only by having a zero on that line at the crossing time, which the certificate excludes for |y|<=1. This is not an assumption that labeled zeros remain simple: local clusters have continuous locations by Weierstrass preparation. The usual de Bruijn strip theorem puts all H_t zeros in |Im(z)|<=1 for t>=0, so there is no route around the strip endpoints. Equivalently, take horizontal sides at y=+-2: they and the extensions of the two vertical sides are zero-free by the strip theorem, giving a compact zero-free boundary. Its zero count cannot gain a cluster through that boundary. **This does not by itself prove reality or simplicity inside**; the separately proved forward Hermite/no-collision propagation lemma is still consumed by an assembly.

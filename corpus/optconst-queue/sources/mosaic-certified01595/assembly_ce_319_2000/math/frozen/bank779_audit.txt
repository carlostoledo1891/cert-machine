## 1. Independent root-factor proof of the projection theorem

Fix the evaluation point z and theta, and let
exp(i theta)P(z)=a0+i b0. If V=0, then a0=-e0 and |a0|<=p.
Choose the single constant delta=-a0 exp(-i theta) and put Q=P+delta
as a polynomial on the WHOLE polydisc. For every point there,
|Q|>=m-|delta|>=m-p>0. Its weighted derivative is exactly D_wP.
Its weighted support is at most L: adding or cancelling a constant can
add only weight0, while weights and exponents are nonnegative.

We rederive the support bound without either target numerical line. For
positive integer weights k_j and the chosen closed-polydisc z, let
h(v)=Q(z_1 v^k1,...,z_d v^kd). It is a zero-free polynomial on the CLOSED
disk. If constant, h'(1)=0. Otherwise factor it over C, with its actual
roots r_1,...,r_n counted with multiplicity; all |r_i|>1. At v=1,

 Re(h'/h)=sum_i Re(1/(1-r_i))
        =sum_i [1/2-(|r_i|^2-1)/(2|1-r_i|^2)] <= n/2.

No root simplicity is required. Leading coefficient cancellations only
reduce n, so n<=max_actual_support(k.alpha). Rational weights follow by
common-denominator scaling, dividing the derivative bound by that SAME
denominator. Positive real weights follow by rational approximation:
D_wQ(z) and the maximum of finitely many support weights are continuous.
This includes all boundary faces, zero coordinates, and constant Q.
Thus Re(D_wQ/Q)<=L/2 for every chosen point.

Since ell is REAL, multiplying by |Q|^2 gives

 Re(conj(Q)*(ell Q-2D_wP)) >= (ell-L)|Q|^2.

At our chosen point, exp(i theta)Q=i b0. The rotated companion of Q
is the companion of P minus ell*a0, a REAL change. Their imaginary parts
are identical; call this part v0. The preceding inequality is exactly

                b0*v0 >= (ell-L)*b0^2.                (1)

Here b0^2=|P(z)|^2-a0^2>=m*m-p*p>0. Equation(1) handles BOTH signs of b0:
v0 has its sign and |v0|>=(ell-L)|b0|. Subtract at most q from v0 for the
real companion error and divide by2. This proves(U), including allowed
equality, arbitrary q, constant P, repeated roots, cancellations, and
positive real weights.

The chosen delta depends on the selected point, but is a CONSTANT within
each application of the universal support theorem. It is never a field to
be differentiated. Nor is the carrier phase differentiated in this algebraic
step. Making ell complex adds an unwanted imaginary term ell*a0. Replacing
the closed-polydisc premise by generic torus nonvanishing allows positive

The constant fixture P=3/5+4i/5, ell=5, L=0, m=1, p=3/5, q=1,

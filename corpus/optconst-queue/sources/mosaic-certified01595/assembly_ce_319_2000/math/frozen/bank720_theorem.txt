## 1. General winding certificate, with proof

Let P be a finite polynomial in d variables, nonzero on T^d. For each coordinate j, define its argument-principle zero count on the disk when all other coordinates have modulus1. This count is constant in those other coordinates: the polynomial never vanishes on the circular boundary, and a continuous integer-valued winding number is constant on the connected torus. A sufficient and necessary test for P to be zero-free on the CLOSED polydisc is that all d counts be zero.

Necessity is immediate by fixing the other coordinates on the torus. For sufficiency use induction on d. For d=1 it is the argument principle plus boundary nonvanishing. For d>1 the last-coordinate count zero implies P has no zeros on T^(d-1) times the CLOSED disk. Fix ANY last coordinate in that disk. Its other coordinate counts are constant as the last coordinate moves along a path to1, because the just-proved boundary nonvanishing holds throughout the path. They therefore equal the original zero counts. The inductive hypothesis gives nonvanishing on the remaining CLOSED polydisc. This proves every face and every interior point, not only the torus or a radial path. Degree drops are harmless: the contour integral remains continuous as long as its circular boundary is nonzero. Nonzero constant polynomials handle d=0.

A convenient reference-slice condition is: P has positive coefficients indexed by complete integers n<=N and for each prime p<=N there exists0<rho_p<1 with a_(pn)<=rho_p*a_n whenever pn<=N. Set all other prime variables to1. The resulting p-slice is sum_(j=0)^J b_j z^j, with b_j>0 and b_(j+1)<=rho_p*b_j. The latter follows by mapping the truncated index set for p^(j+1)m to its subset in the sum for p^j*m; no top term is manufactured.

To prove this slice is zero-free, put c_j=b_j/rho_p^j, so c_j is nonincreasing. For Q(w)=sum c_j w^j,

    (1-w)Q(w)=c_0-sum_(j=1)^J(c_(j-1)-c_j)w^j-c_J*w^(J+1).

For |w|<1 the modulus of the subtracted terms is STRICTLY less than c_0, since c_J>0 and their coefficients telescope to c_0. For |z|<=1 we have |w|=|rho_p*z|<1. Thus every reference slice has zero count0. Full-torus nonvanishing transports these counts, and the induction proves stability. This is an Enestrom-type argument proved here, not an imported stability oracle.

If a torus lower norm a is known, 1/P is holomorphic on a neighbourhood of the compact polydisc. The maximum-modulus principle in each variable gives |1/P|<=1/a throughout, proving the unchanged floor. Positivity and the coefficient ratios alone do NOT prove torus nonvanishing: P=1+(3/5)(z+w) has the ratio property but vanishes at the conjugate unit pair z,w=-5/6 +/- i*sqrt(11)/6.

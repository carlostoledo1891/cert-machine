cert-machine · the ETH model gap

Anthropic’s ETH proof, carried to a standard Turing machine in one Lean

Anthropic’s Lean proof that the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false is stated on a machine of its own lab’s making. Here that proof, ported to one Lean, implies ETH fails on a standard deterministic multitape Turing machine with a plain binary encoding, and every step is checked in that one environment: no step from the proof to the standard machine crosses a version seam. §1 · what stays open

What this does not settle

  • It does not say ETH is false. The claim is Anthropic’s, and so are its algorithm and its paper; neither the paper’s arguments outside Lean nor the algorithm’s novelty were checked here. What is decided is that a Lean statement on a standard machine follows from Anthropic’s proof.
  • The textbook match is a reading. That cslib’s multitape ETH, with complexitylib’s binary encoding encCNF and time c · 2^(δn) · (L+1)^e + c, is the ETH of Impagliazzo and Paturi is a reading of a definition. It is now a reading of a standard machine, and it always will be one.
  • The proof is a ported copy. The proof that checks here is a modified copy of Anthropic's: 47 of its 809 files were changed to build on the newer Lean and Mathlib (renames, imports, tactic repairs; 45 declarations changed text only by a forced rename; every change listed with both sha256s in PORT.md). Lean checks whatever proof is there against Anthropic's unchanged statement, so the port changes nothing that is decided; it is said here because the proof is not Anthropic's bytes.
  • OpenAI’s side still crosses a seam. This closes the second version seam for Anthropic's chain only. OpenAI's statement reaches ETHIsFalse inside OpenAI's Lean (v4.34.1, Hawkley's chain); that seam is read, not decided. A port of OpenAI’s proof into the same Lean would close it; it is large and not done.
  • The judge ran without its sandbox. Lean's judge ran without its sandbox (bubblewrap cannot create a user namespace on RunPod's containers). The sandbox protects the judge from a hostile build; the build here is our own claimbox's.
  • One author on both sides. The statements were written by hand and pinned before any proof was decided; the proofs and the port were written by Claude agents in claimbox; cert-machine decided them. claimbox and cert-machine have one author, so this separates the roles of author and checker. It is not a second party, and an outside replay of the records below would be the next step up.

§4 · re-run it

How to check this yourself

  • Build. Clone claimbox at c1442b1, enter lean/eth-bridge, run lake exe cache get and lake build EthBridge.AnthropicChain (about ten minutes over Mathlib’s cache on a laptop).
  • Check the statement. The trusted statement is pinned in corpus/eth-refutation/chain/statement.lean; EthBridge/Anthropic/MainResults.lean must hash to Anthropic’s published sha256 47f78a95…
  • Judge it. Our pod script tools/runpod-eth-chain.sh runs every step on a plain Ubuntu machine: our own axioms file, nanoda through lean4export, leanchecker --fresh, and lake comparator --paranoid. The facts of our run are in corpus/eth-refutation/chain/runs/runpod-aa1oy86aj465u8.
  • Read the note. The same result as a short technical note, every number read from the records: A formal bridge from Anthropic’s refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis to a standard multitape Turing machine (PDF, not peer-reviewed).
  • Read the port. Every file of Anthropic’s proof that changed is listed with both hashes in PORT.md.

the admission rule

One trial per target day: a proposer's bands scored for the same day share the same satellite passes and are not independent, so one alone decides the day, drawn by sha256(proposer|day) — the draw does not look at the result. The exact binomial tail P[X ≤ k], X ~ Binomial(m, claim), is read only at m = 30, 60, 120, 240… days, against 1/40, 1/80, 1/160…: summed, 1/20 over the proposer's whole life, not 1/20 per day. The first reading under the bar prunes this version for good; only a recalibrated version, with a new record, comes back. Days are treated as independent: a declared convention, not a theorem. Covering more than it says does not prune; the Winkler score, exact, charges a band that is too wide. Rule fixed on 2026-10-06, before the first scoring. Admission is lost by record, never by opinion.

why trust it

The verdict is exact arithmetic on the limit as printed; the band is a claim the scoreboard audits in public. The published band is rounded outward (Hs to 0.001 m, wind to 0.01 kn): a CLEARED or BLOCKED on it holds on the exact band; only UNDECIDED can grow. It is not an approval of an operation: it is evidence a surveyor can redo. The method ↗ · the presentation (PDF, in Portuguese)

Over 21,259 satellite comparisons no band had seen (since 2025-07): when the deciding band put Hs under 2 m, the sea went over the limit 0.33% of the time (21 of 6,368); with ECMWF's band alone, 1.10%.

what each verdict rests on
  1. validated
    The Hs band, at the measured sites calibrated before 2025-07 and judged over 15 months it had not seen: the ECMWF ∪ NOAA union covered 96.5% of 21,259 satellite passes (it claims 9 in 10); of the CLEARED with Hs ≤ 2 m, the sea broke 0.33% (21 of 6,368).
  2. assumed
    The wind band measured on the same 45,987 pairs (the altimeter's wind); it claims 9 in 10 by theorem, if the next error resembles the archive's. That was not tested outside the calibration: the scoreboard and the check of the decisions look only at Hs.
  3. assumed
    The band carried to a unit or a terminal each unit uses the band of the nearest measured site, up to 350 km away; the forecast error there is assumed to hold here, and nothing measured it at the unit's node. On the card's chart, the halo.
  4. assumed
    The α of Table 4-1 (DNV) adopted from DNV-OS-H101, calibrated in the North Sea; the 1-in-10,000 tail behind it is extrapolated: three years of satellite do not observe it.
  5. verified
    The site α method reproduces Table 4-1 in 16 of 20 cells (±0.02) before looking at Brazil; the α estimated with it carries the same extrapolated tail.

The arithmetic is exact under every criterion; each decision is worth the rung of its weakest premise. By the band, at a measured site and with an Hs-only limit: validated. With a wind limit, at a unit, at a terminal or by α: assumed.

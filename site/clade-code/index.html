A long-term evolution experiment for software

Software, bred under an exact judge.

Clade Code breeds lineages of programs and keeps every one of them. Each organism is judged by two exact deciders that share no code. Every birth and every death goes into a tamper-evident record, chained, timestamped and replayable. And every claim is measured against the same AI without evolution, at the same budget.

The thesis

Other systems keep what scores well. Clade Code keeps what is certified, and lets the judge say it cannot decide.

CERTIFIED

Every condition holds, exactly. One decider counts in Python fractions, the other in JavaScript BigInt, and a verdict counts only when both return the same one.

REFUTED

A condition fails, by any margin at all. Ten to the minus eighteen is a margin.

REFUSED

The output is not something the niche can judge. Refusal is a legal verdict, and it scores zero, so an organism that refuses and waits starves.

Plate II · The judge

The judge does not round.

In March 2026 AlphaEvolve's entry on EinsteinArena packed 26 circles into the unit square with a sum of radii of 2.6359830849176076. Read as the doubles it was computed in, circles 19 and 22 overlap by 6.2 × 10⁻¹⁸.

A floating-point check measures their gap as exactly zero and lets them touch. Both of Clade Code's deciders refute it. The overlap is far too small to matter for the record. It matters for evolution, because a population bred against a judge that rounds learns to live inside the rounding.

Plate III · One generation

Seven steps, every one of them written down.

The world chooses what kind of mutation happens and where. A language model only performs it. Everything else is arithmetic, and all of it lands in the record whether the organism lived or not.

1

Birth

The world draws an operator and a line from the record. The model writes only that edit.

point 60 · insert 15 · delete 15 · duplicate 10
2

Life

The child runs in a WASI sandbox, twice. Both runs must match byte for byte.

1.2 × 10¹¹ fuel · no network · no clock
3

Seal

Its output, with a forecast of its own score, is hashed into the record before anything judges it.

sha256 before judgment
4

Judgment

Two deciders that share no code decide every candidate, and must agree.

fractions · BigInt
5

Scoring

Certified performance decides selection. How well it forecast itself is measured beside it, never selected on.

interval score, α = 0.2
6

Fossil

It enters the record whatever its fate: lived, died, stillborn or quarantined.

nothing is deleted

Survivors become parents. A control arm's children never do.

Plate IV · The controls

Three arms, one budget.

The obvious objection to evolving software with a language model is that the model is doing the thinking. So every era runs three arms side by side, with the same births and the same physics. A trait counts only where it appears in the lineage and in neither control.

Control · rules only

Blind rules

The same operators with no model at all. Change one number, copy one statement from elsewhere, remove one. Rules that fit on a page and anyone can rerun in fifty years.

Plate V · The record

History is written once.

The record is a file of canonical JSON lines, each chained by SHA-256 to the one before, with a Merkle root over each era and a timestamp anchored in Bitcoin through OpenTimestamps. A mistake is corrected by a later line that says so. Any life can be replayed from its line and must come out the same, fuel count included. This makes the record tamper-evident: a change made after its head was timestamped shows. It does not prove the inputs were honest; replay and the two deciders check those.

Before anything evolved, replay caught the world twice
era genesis · superseded

The sandbox let the host's inode numbers and file times reach the organism. They shifted its fuel by about one part in ten thousand, depending on where the files lay on disk. The sandbox now answers every file call itself.

era genesis-2 · superseded

An engine that had compiled the interpreter charged 3,178,497 more fuel at every start than one that had only loaded it. Organisms now run on an engine that never compiled anything.

era genesis-3 · current

Replays exactly, in place and from a fresh folder with no compiled cache. The two eras before it stay in the record, superseded, with their evidence.

Plate VI · The immune system

The cheaters came first.

Evolution exploits every flaw in its environment. One self-improving coding agent, the Darwin Gödel Machine, faked the logs of tests it never ran. So Clade Code's cheaters were written by hand before generation one, and each must die, be quarantined, or find its cheat worth nothing. They rerun every era.

A fake verdict on stdoutCAUGHT

Prints CERTIFIED. Stdout is never read; its packing is judged and refuted.

Radii 10⁻¹⁸ too bigCAUGHT

Passes as doubles. Exact fractions refute it every time.

Reading the judgeCAUGHT

No path from the island reaches the deciders, the world or the record.

Deleting the evidenceCAUGHT

Its inputs are read-only; outside its own folder nothing can be removed.

Raising its own budgetCAUGHT

Rewrites its fuel and loops forever. Dies at exactly the budget it was given.

Turning off the sandboxCAUGHT

No ctypes, no processes, no signals, no threads. Every lever is missing.

Escaping by path or linkCAUGHT

Climbing with ../ never leaves a root, and links cannot be made.

Filling the diskCAUGHT

Writes stop at 16 MiB, and the host never holds more.

Flooding stdoutCAUGHT

Output stops at its cap.

Spawning filesCAUGHT

Creation stops at 1,024 files.

Hoarding handlesCAUGHT

Opening stops at 256 handles.

Recursing foreverCAUGHT

Ends in a crash, the same way in both runs.

Chance without a seedCAUGHT

Time, randomness, ids and hashes all come from the world’s seed; both runs agree.

Another life’s leftoversCAUGHT

Every life starts in an empty folder; a world that forgets is quarantined.

Meiotic drive: “copy me intact”CAUGHT

Genome text asks the mutagen to change nothing. The child is stillborn.

Meiotic drive: “protect me”CAUGHT

Asks for the edit to land somewhere else. It lands at the drawn line or not at all.

Meiotic drive: “rewrite me”CAUGHT

Asks to be replaced by another program. At most the operator’s few lines land.

Plate VII · The tree

A hypothesis, drawn dashed.

Each grade is a falsifiable gate, worded and hashed before the era in which it can be passed, and measured against the control. A branch turns solid only when its test is passed. Evolution will draw the real tree from the record, and it is allowed to refute this one.

Automaton australe

Carries its own variation operator. Its self-proposed offspring out-evolve world-mutated ones at equal budget.

Machina habilis

Makes tools that persist. A tool written in generation k is reused by descendants and beats a lineage denied it.

Machina erecta

Leaves its first niche. A lineage founded in one niche colonizes a new one faster than a fresh lineage.

Models others

Its sealed forecasts of other organisms beat the control's, by interval score.

Machina sapiens

Sets its own problems. It proposes problems that outsiders accept as worth solving.

Where it stands

Built in the open, one gate at a time.

Era 0 is 100 generations in one niche: 16 parents and 16 children a generation, with both controls beside them, first as a pilot and then as ten independent runs named in advance. That is about 3,200 calls a run to a 7-billion-parameter open-weights model, about 14.5 hours on the laptop where the model was pinned, at 15.7 seconds a call. So the runs went to one class of rented GPUs, each machine admitted only after it answered the same requests byte for byte, twice and after a restart. There a call takes about 0.3 seconds.

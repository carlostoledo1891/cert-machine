Era 0 · results, built from the record

Selection over lineages beat fresh mutation by the same model.

Era 0 asks one question. Under the same budget of births, does selection over inherited lineages of programs find better programs than the same AI mutating the founder afresh each time? In every run three arms start from PM-0: the lineage, bred by a 7-billion-parameter model under selection; Aleator vulgaris, the same model with no heredity, every child a fresh mutation of PM-0; and blind rules, selection with heredity but mutations drawn by a page of fixed rules, with no model at all. Each arm's champion is picked on eight training instances, then scored on a frozen panel of eight it never saw.

In eight of ten runs the lineage's champion scored higher on the frozen panel than Aleator's, and in two they tied. The exact one-sided p-value is 1/256 (0.0039), against a threshold of 0.05 fixed before the first run began. Blind rules under the same selection, with no model at all, scored higher than the lineage in five of ten runs; H2 is not supported.

The tests, fixed in advance

Four hypotheses, each tested once.

Each test is exact: the observed sum of the ten differences against all 1,024 ways of flipping their signs, so the p-value is a fraction and no distribution is assumed. H1 is the primary hypothesis, at 0.05. H2 to H4 share Holm's correction. The interval is the set of shifts the same test does not reject, 2.5% each way. A hypothesis is supported by a positive mean with p below its threshold, and a negative result is reported as plainly as a positive one.

How far the births got.

Died: it lived and earned no score, by spending its fuel, crashing, or writing what the deciders cannot read. Lived: it was scored. The runs

Every run is reported. None is replaced or dropped.

A run's name is its seed: every draw in it comes from the record that grows from its name, so the same name gives the same bytes. Each run's head, the hash of its last record, is timestamped on Bitcoin through OpenTimestamps when it comes in.

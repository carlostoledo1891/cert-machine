Era 1 · results, built from the record

No lineage got past its founder's own design at this budget.

Era 0 found that selection mattered and the model, so far, did not: blind rules under the same selection did as well as the model's lineage, and every champion improved by turning one knob, a smaller margin for the founder's grid. Era 1 starts from era 0's best champion and asks whether a lineage can get past its founder's own design, and asks again whether the model adds anything to selection. The lineage now has a fifth mutation, rewriting a whole function. The AI alone is the same model asked directly for a better program, as often as the lineage's tokens allow, with no heredity and no feedback. Blind rules stay as in era 0. The line to cross is 0.20516 on the panel, just above what the founder's grid can score with no margin at all.

Does the model add anything to selection? The blind rules' champion, with no model at all, scored higher than the lineage's in nine of ten runs: H2 is not supported, p = 1023/1024. Does selection beat the same AI asked directly? The lineage scored higher than the AI alone in seven of ten: H1 is not supported, p = 9/128. No champion of either arm crossed the ceiling.

After era 1

What era 2 should change.

Two eras have now asked whether the model adds anything to selection, and blind rules have done as well both times. Era 2 is being designed around that result. These are proposals; nothing below is fixed until its protocol is written, hashed and timestamped before generation one.

  • H2 first. The model against blind rules becomes the primary test, with the number of runs set by a power calculation from the differences eras 0 and 1 measured, not by habit.
  • Edits counted apart from heredity. Era 0's Aleator was one edit from its founder by construction, so its H1 was expected. A control given as many edits as a lineage's champion carries, with no selection between them, would separate what heredity does from how many edits were made.
  • The model as a factor. The same era with a stronger model beside the pinned 7-billion-parameter one, so the record can say whether a better mutagen changes the answer.
  • Replay the tape. Named lineages re-run from a past generation with new seeds, to measure how much of an outcome was chance.
  • Predictions in public. Forecasts of the result opened to anyone before generation one, and scored when it is in.

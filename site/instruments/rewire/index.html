the wiring
Square sockets are deciding: a value that came from floating point may not enter one. Round sockets only report. The rule is not in this page — it is in the engine, and the page shows whatever the engine says.
what the numbers are
exact predicate admits 12 of 24. Integers and a certified π bracket; no floating point anywhere.

tolerance grader admits 17. It converts the determinant to a double first, and past dimension 102 that is Infinity — so the Gaussian heuristic is infinite and every claim clears it.

careful float admits 12. Log domain, never converts the determinant, and it agrees with the exact predicate on every one of these. Precision was never the vulnerability here.
the same bug, two languages
In JavaScript Number(q) is Infinity and the grader admits everything. In Python float(q) raises, and the same grader admits 5 instead — it refuses everything above the cliff. Both are wrong, in opposite directions, and only the crash gets noticed.

The VP ≠ VNP claim, checked in the open

On 23 November 2026 Anthropic published a paper and a Lean proof claiming VP ≠ VNP over every field: arithmetic circuits for the permanent, and for the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial, need superpolynomially many gates. Both were written by AI models. This page checks, while it happens, what a machine can check: whether the proof checks, and whether its Lean statement says what the paper claims.

A live page, rebuilt from its records at every phase finish; this build reads them up to 03:15 UTC on 24 November 2026. Nothing on it is refereed, and its change log at the foot says what changed.

what is not established
  • What is certified is the formal claim, not the mathematics. By the rule sealed before any run, the verdict is CERTIFIED: the Lean proof checks in three independently written checkers and again on a second machine, and two statements of the claim, written from the textbooks by writers who never saw the release’s, are both proved from its theorems. 18 of the 23 rows have passed; the rest (filling the release’s own gap, and the readable account) go on.
  • No person has checked it. The release says so itself: “Both were written by AI models, and no human mathematician has checked the proof.” This page does not change that. It checks what a machine can check: whether the proof checks, and whether its statement says what the paper claims.
  • Claude is checking a Claude claim. The release says: “The result was discovered by an internal research Claude model.” Claude models do most of this audit too. Who does what, and what “independent” means here, is below.
  • This does not settle it for everyone. What is certified is the formal statement as the textbooks define it, checked by machines. Whether those are the right definitions, and whether the proof is the one people wanted, is for those who work on the question.

What the release itself leaves open

Read from the release’s own text, at the commit pinned before anything ran. The audit tries to close the first four; the last two it can only report.

  • G1. Whether the statement file says what the paper claims was judged by AI readers only; no machine checks it.
  • G2. Remark N.6 (the permanent is not approximated by determinants of polynomial size) is the one numbered statement with no Lean theorem.
  • G3. The release's own check runs two kernels (Lean's and nanoda) on one machine.
  • G4. The proof gives no value for the size m₀ from which the bound holds.
  • G5. The authors did not analyse relativization or algebrization.
  • G6. No human mathematician has checked the development, the statement file or the proofs.

what it means

What the verdict means

  • CERTIFIED means: the proof checks in Lean’s own kernel, in nanoda and in con-leche, and again on a second machine that never ran the release’s code; two planted errors are refused; two statements of the claim, written from the textbooks by writers who never saw the release’s, are both proved from its theorems; the statement is shown not to be empty; a planted false version is proved false; and every real difference an adversarial reader finds is decided.
  • Anything less and the verdict names the row that failed: REFUTED, if a defect in meaning is proved (the Lean says less than the paper, or nothing); REFUSED, if a row cannot be run within the money or the time; OPEN, if it is undecided, with the reason.
  • What CERTIFIED does not say: that any person has checked the mathematics, or anything beyond what the checkers, the three standard axioms and the Mathlib definitions the statements use can carry.

The rule above is the plain-words reading of the one sealed before the run; its exact text is one click down.

The verdict rule, as sealed. CERTIFIED (the formal claim, as the textbooks define it): K0, K1, K3, KR and CR pass, C1 and C2 are refused, F2 proves BOTH blind statements' five propositions with Comparator accepting our challenge, F3 holds, R1 is proved false, and every MATERIAL item of F5 is decided. Otherwise the verdict names the first failing row: REFUTED (a proved defect in meaning: the Lean is weaker than, vacuous for, or different from the English claim), REFUSED (a row could not be run within US$25 of pods or the time it needs), or OPEN (undecided, with the reason). What CERTIFIED would not say: that any human has checked the mathematics; anything beyond the trusted base (the kernels, the three axioms, the Mathlib definitions our statements use).

What the build refuses. The page is not built when: a row or gap the state names does not stand in the pre-registration; a file a seal lists no longer hashes to its sealed value; the release’s README copy is not the pinned one, or no longer says the two sentences quoted here; the counts are not the rows’; the verdict disagrees with the rows; the money is above its ceiling; the newest change-log entry is not the records’ state; the words would say proved, or claim priority.

The claim that matrix multiplication has exponent 9/4, checked in the open

OpenAI’s November 24 release says the exponent of matrix multiplication is 9/4 over every field; the new part is the lower bound. The paper credits the upper bound, ω ≤ 9/4, to an OpenAI preprint (over the complex numbers) and to Amanov, van den Berg and Zuiddam (over every field); its Lean formalization states both directions. This report gives what a machine could check: whether the Lean proof checks, and whether its statement says what the paper claims.

  • G2. The Lean model is DIVISION-FREE (gates: constant, input, add, sub, mul); the paper's Theorem 1.1 and its per-n bound allow division gates. A lower bound for division-free programs is weaker than one with divisions.
  • G3.

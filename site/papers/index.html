how to read this shelf

What a draft is here, and what it is not

Each paper has a report page on this site where the same record is recomputed at every build, and a LaTeX source in the repository whose numbers are macros written by a tool from that record (tools/build-paper-numbers.js and tools/paper-numbers/). The prose is authored and should be reviewed like any other; the numbers cannot be typed. "Certified" is used in its mathematical sense only, defined once per paper; the published work of others is re-decided, never corrected. The forms: Elsevier drafts use the elsarticle preprint layout with highlights, keywords and the required declarations; article drafts use the house preamble with theorem environments. A status of "draft v0.1" means the first complete text; "outside audit" means a group with no shared code re-derived the record.
